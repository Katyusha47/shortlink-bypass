Community Feedback & Responsible Disclosure

We welcome feedback from developers, security researchers, and everyday users. Whether you noticed that a shortlink provider has updated their redirect mechanisms or you want to suggest a new service adapter, your contributions help keep the resolution engine accurate and reliable.

Report a Broken Provider

URL shorteners frequently change their DOM structures, query parameters, or intermediate redirect pages. If an adapter in our Supported Providers list is failing or reporting inaccurate results, please report:

  • The shortlink provider name (e.g. Bitly, MediaFire, TinyURL).
  • An example shortened URL that failed (ensure it does not contain private or personal information).
  • The expected destination versus the returned error.

Security & Vulnerability Disclosure

We treat security with the utmost urgency. If you discover a potential Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF) bypass, DNS rebinding loophole, denial-of-service vector, or any other vulnerability:

  • Please practice responsible disclosure by contacting our security team before publishing any exploit details.
  • Provide detailed reproduction steps and affected URL patterns.
  • We will investigate and deploy mitigations promptly.
