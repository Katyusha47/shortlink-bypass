Supported Providers & Compatibility

Our resolution engine uses a modular, provider-based architecture. We believe in complete transparency: we never claim universal compatibility, and we clearly specify the operational status and resolution strategy for each supported service.

Security & Compliance Policy

Universal Shortlink Bypasser only resolves links through legitimate, publicly accessible mechanisms. The engine does not bypass authentication, CAPTCHA challenges, payment requirements, or private access controls. If a provider activates an interactive human verification challenge or anti-bot barrier, our system accurately returns a blocked status rather than attempting circumvention.
